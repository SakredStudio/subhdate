## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

ShubhDate is a Vedic panchang and muhurta app that shows you the right moment and the reason why.

TODAY'S PANCHANG, FOR YOUR CITY
Sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset. Tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana with the exact time each one ends. Month (amanta and purnimanta), paksha, Vikram and Shaka samvat, Gujarati Vikram Samvat, ritu and ayana. Rahu kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika, Abhijit, Brahma muhurta, durmuhurta, varjyam, amrit kaal and pradosha. Choghadiya and hora for day and night. The day's yogas and doshas. A ready-to-read sankalpa in Devanagari and IAST.

Everything is computed from the Moon and the Sun for your own city and your own sunrise: New Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Toronto, London, Leicester, Nairobi, Singapore, Sydney and more than 3,500 other cities, offline. Diaspora dates can differ from India by a day; the app tells you when they do.

FIND A MUHURTA
Choose an event, a date range and a city, and ShubhDate ranks the days and the windows within each day: wedding (vivaha), house entry (griha pravesh), engagement, vehicle purchase, business opening, naming, first feeding, mundan, travel, new job, exam or interview, bhoomi puja. Every window lists which classical rules hold in its favour and which count against it. Add your birth details to include Tara bala and Chandra bala; they stay on your phone.

FESTIVALS BY RULE, NOT BY LIST
Diwali, Dhanteras, Karwa Chauth, Govardhan, Bhai Dooj, Devutthani Ekadashi, Makar Sankranti, Vasant Panchami, Maha Shivaratri, Holi and every Ekadashi are computed for your city from the panchang rules, with the puja window and the moonrise where the tradition defines one.

THE MUHURTA REPORT (optional purchase)
A printable report for one event: the five best days, the full panchanga of each day, the sankalpa for each day, and a page for your pandit that lists every rule that applied with its source, in Devanagari and your script. Print it to PDF from your phone. The free app shows the cover, the best window and its sankalpa; the full report is a one-time purchase, or unlimited with the yearly Family plan.

HONEST BY DESIGN
• Positions from a validated ephemeris; 7,160 checks against Swiss Ephemeris, 100% within tolerance (Moon within 6 arc-seconds).
• 248 of 248 fields matched on 17 hand-read Drik Panchang pages; 73 of 73 kala windows within a minute.
• Lahiri ayanamsa, sunrise-to-sunrise day, both amanta and purnimanta months, Gujarati Vikram Samvat.
• Rules from Muhurta Chintamani, Dharmasindhu, Kalaprakashika (via B.V. Raman) and Brihat Samhita; every rule carries its source, and rows the research could not verify are marked so.
• The calculation engine is open source (MIT) so your pandit can check how any value was computed.
• Treat the results as a shortlist. Your family purohit knows your gotra, sutak and kula-achara, which no app can.

LANGUAGES
Hindi, Gujarati, English and Hinglish in full; Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Kannada interface in beta.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Birth details never leave your phone. Works offline after the first open. Optional festival reminders, when you turn them on, store only your push endpoint, city coordinates and language.

ShubhDate is made by Sakred Studio in Toronto for families in India and across the diaspora. It does not predict outcomes; it reads the panchang the way a careful pandit would and shows you its reasons.
