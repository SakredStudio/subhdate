Refund policy

ShubhDate by Sakred Studio (17899181 Canada Inc.), Toronto. Effective 2026-11-14. Applies to the Muhurta Report, the Namakarana Report and the Family plan, bought on shubhdate.sakredstudio.ca or in the Android app.

The short version

  • Every report shows a free preview before you pay, so you can see what you are buying.
  • A report is digital and unlocks the moment you pay. Once it has unlocked, it cannot be returned.
  • If something went wrong, you get your money back: write to us within 7 days.

When we refund in full

  • The report did not unlock after you paid, and we cannot unlock it for you.
  • The report does not work: it will not open, or the PDF will not download, and we cannot fix it.
  • You were charged twice, or bought the same report twice by mistake.
  • Family plan: within 7 days of paying, as long as you have not yet opened a full report with it.

A report that unlocked and works is not refundable, including when you have changed your mind after reading it. The Family plan bought on the website is one payment for one year and does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel later; we do not refund part of a year.

How to ask

  • Website purchases (paid through Stripe): email sakredfamily@gmail.com with the date, the product, and the email address on your Stripe receipt. We reply within 3 business days.
  • Android app purchases (paid through Google Play): ask Google Play from your Google Play order history, or email us and we will refund it from our side within Google Play's time limits.
  • Please write to us before disputing the charge with your bank. A dispute takes weeks; a refund from us usually reaches your card in 5 to 10 business days.

When a refund goes through, the report or plan locks again in the browser or app that bought it.

Your copy

Each paid report is a licensed copy for your personal and family use. Every page carries a short reference that links the copy to its purchase; it is not your name and contains no personal details. Please do not resell or post paid reports.

Outside these rules we may, once per person, refund as a goodwill gesture. That is our choice in each case, not a right.
