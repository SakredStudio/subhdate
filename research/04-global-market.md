# SubhDate rebuild — Research 04: Should ShubhDate open to a global (non-Hindu) audience?

Date: 2026-10-16. Question: keep ShubhDate India + diaspora only, or also serve people who do not know Vedic astrology?

Method: ~68 WebSearch (standard + extended) and WebFetch lookups. Every number is tagged **[VERIFIED]** (read on the primary/official page or a named first-tier outlet quoting it), **[SECONDARY]** (third-party tracker, aggregator, trade blog, or a search-result summary of a primary source) or **[UNVERIFIED]** (single weak source, self-reported, or could not be traced). **[GAP]** = could not be found; what to do about it is stated inline. Blocked/paywalled at fetch layer: Sensor Tower and Appfigures dashboards (login), Statista figures (asterisked), Google Trends (not indexed by search), Chinese app-store analytics (QuestMobile paywalled).

## 0. Ten findings that decide the question

1. **The money outside India is in relationship horoscopes + psychic chat, not in "good day" lookups.** Nebula (Obrio) ran at **$50M ARR with 30M users in Jan 2024** and ~98% of its queries are relationship questions [VERIFIED https://thenextweb.com/news/ukrainian-startup-astrology-app-nebula-thriving-despite-war]. CHANI (~$14M/yr est.) and Co-Star are the US #1/#2 grossing astrology apps in Q1 2026 [SECONDARY https://www.statista.com/statistics/1451664/top-horoscope-apps-us-market-revenue/]. The muhurta-shaped need (pick a date for a wedding/move/launch) is served in the West by *numerology listicles* on The Knot, not by apps.
2. **North America converts ~4x better and pays ~2.5x more than India/SEA per download.** RevenueCat 2026 medians: D35 download-to-paid **2.8% NA vs 0.7% IN/SEA**; trial-to-paid **34.2% vs 15.2%**; median yearly price **$39.99 vs $18.32**; revenue per install at D60 **$0.55 vs $0.11**; year-1 LTV per payer **$32 vs $14** [VERIFIED https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps]. That is the entire pull toward "global".
3. **The push against "global" is regulatory and reputational, and it is current.** The FTC obtained a TRO (N.D. Cal., reported 1 Jul 2026) against the Genesis Tech group whose products include **Nebula**, alleging hidden auto-renewals, charges after cancellation and ROSCA violations; the named products took "nearly a quarter billion dollars" early 2023–mid 2025 [VERIFIED https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/07/01/ftc-takes-action-to-halt-allegedly-deceptive-subscription-schemes/]. Nebula's Trustpilot is **1.3/5, 96% one-star (n=116)** [VERIFIED https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/nebula.app]. Faladdin's founder was detained in Turkey in Jul 2025 under the anti-divination Law 677 [VERIFIED https://www.turkishminute.com/2025/07/16/turkey-detains-faladdin-founder-seizes-assets-over-ai-powered-fortune-telling-gains/]. Apple's Guideline 4.3 literally lists "fortune telling" as a saturated category it does not want more of [VERIFIED https://daringfireball.net/linked/2025/11/26/november-update-to-the-app-store-review-guidelines].
4. **Belief is real but shallow in the West.** Pew (fielded Oct 2024, n=9,593): **30%** of US adults consult astrology/tarot/fortune tellers at least yearly, **27%** believe in astrology, **43% of women 18–49** believe, but only **1%** rely on it "a lot" for major decisions and 20% do it "just for fun" [VERIFIED https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/21/3-in-10-americans-consult-astrology-tarot-cards-or-fortune-tellers/]. UK is far lower: YouGov found **8%** of Britons believe horoscopes predict the future (2015, old) [VERIFIED https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/12731-8-of-Britons-believe-horoscopes-predict-the-future]. "Spiritual but not religious" is **21%** of US adults [VERIFIED https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/02/26/spiritual-and-religious-self-descriptions/].
5. **The Western search demand that matches our engine is "moon phase", not "auspicious date".** Ahrefs estimates **242K US searches/month** for "moon phase today" and moongiant.com takes 131K visits/month from that one keyword [SECONDARY https://ahrefs.com/websites/moongiant.com]. No tool-level volume for "auspicious date" / "good day to move house" could be retrieved [GAP: run Ahrefs/Semrush US+UK+CA for the 8 queries listed in §1.4]. The Moonly analogue (lunar calendar + rituals, plain-English copy) claims 6M+ downloads over ~5 years and third-party trackers put it at roughly **$200K/month** [SECONDARY https://www.bustle.com/life/moonly-astrology-app-review ; https://app.sensortower.com/overview/1489889871?country=us].
6. **East Asia has the real "auspicious day" app culture, and it is closed to us.** Chinese 中华万年历 claims "3.8亿" users and shows 1.4亿 downloads on 应用宝 [SECONDARY https://sj.qq.com/appdetail/cn.etouch.ecalendar ; https://bbs.fcgvisa.com/t/3-8-app/57675]; Korea's Jeomsin has **17–19M cumulative downloads**, 1.13M MAU (Aug 2024) and a ₩1.4T (2024) fortune market behind it [SECONDARY https://www.seoulz.com/korea-saju-industry/]; **49%** of Tokyo-area couples weighed rokuyō for their 2024 wedding and 30.6% chose a taian day [SECONDARY https://news.mynavi.jp/article/20230722-2729753/ citing Zexy]. Chinese Android stores require a Chinese legal entity and ICP/app filing [SECONDARY https://www.hicom-asia.com/launch-app-china-app-stores-icp-filing/]; Korea and Japan are language + incumbent problems, not engine problems.
7. **One engine can compute almost every almanac in §3.** Rokuyō is (lunar month + lunar day) mod 6; Korean 손없는날 is lunar day ending 9/0; Chinese huangli needs sexagenary day pillars + 建除 officers + 28 mansions + 24 solar terms (all from Sun/Moon longitudes + a lunisolar calendar); Thai/Burmese/Sinhala/Nepali almanacs share tithi/nakshatra; Hebrew and Balinese pawukon are pure arithmetic; void-of-course Moon needs planetary longitudes, which Swiss Ephemeris already gives. Technical cost is low; the editorial/legitimacy cost per culture is high.
8. **The diaspora is bigger and richer than it looks and is already paying.** Overseas Indians: **~35.4M (Feb 2025) → 37.3M (Jan 2026)**; US ~5.4M, Canada 1.86–2.9M, UK 1.93M, Australia 0.75–0.85M [SECONDARY https://newsmeter.in/data-stories/from-219-million-in-2012-to-373-million-in-2026-overseas-indian-population-grows-777222 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_diaspora]. Research 01 already found Astrotalk international revenue at ~20% FY25 → 37% FY26, >60% of that US. Diaspora users pay NA prices for a Vedic product with no translation work.
9. **Price ladders.** West: CHANI **$11.99/mo or $107.99/yr** [VERIFIED https://apps.apple.com/bg/app/chani-your-astrology-guide/id1532791252]; Moonly **$3.99/mo, $29.99–$59.99/yr, birth chart $14.99 one-time** [VERIFIED/SECONDARY App Store + Bustle]; Nebula **weekly $2.99/$7.99, quarterly $29.99, annual $39.99/$49.99** (2022 snapshot) [SECONDARY https://adapty.io/paywall-library/nebula-horoscope-astrology/]; The Pattern "Go Deeper+" **$29.99/qtr, $83.99/yr** [SECONDARY https://adapty.io/paywall-library/the-pattern/]; Co-Star: 100% of revenue from à-la-carte IAP [VERIFIED https://www.costarastrology.com/faq]. India: Drik Panchang **₹19/mo, ₹199/yr** ad-free (iOS India), $2.49/yr US [VERIFIED https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hindu-calendar-drik-panchang/id1321271821]; Astrotalk **₹1,176 Cr** FY25 operating revenue, +85%, net profit ₹32.84 Cr after ~₹200 Cr one-offs [VERIFIED https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/astrotalk-revenue-jumps-85-to-rs-1-214-cr-591016].
10. **Verdict (detail in §8):** stay India + diaspora as the product; add a thin, English, SEO-led "Moon & Good-Day" layer on the same engine only after India retention is proven; do not build a separately branded global astrology app. Scenario (c) competes head-on with the FTC-targeted, Apple-4.3-saturated, $50M-ARR-funded segment with a feature (date election) that the Western market does not search for.

## 5. Accessibility for non-Hindu users

- **The sidereal shift is the first-contact failure.** Every explainer on the topic exists because Western users discover their Sun sign moves back one sign (~24° ayanamsa) in Vedic systems; sites that sell Vedic charts to Westerners (Stellr, Nakshatra Jio, Paramarsh, AstroSight) all lead with a "wait, you're not a Scorpio?" page [SECONDARY https://stellr-app.com/en/blog/wait-you-re-not-actually-a-scorpio-what-the-vedic-vs-western ; https://www.nakshatrajio.com/learn/sidereal-vs-tropical-zodiac]. App-review-level evidence ("my sign changed" complaints) was not retrievable [GAP: scan AstroSage/Jyotish-app US reviews on justuseapp]. Design implication: a global mode must never show a Western user a *sign*; show Moon phase, lunar day number, and plain verdicts.
- **What translated well:** Moonly's copy is verdict-first, jargon-free ("New Moon: an energizing time to set goals"; "Full Moon brings resolution and release"; daily tip on "dates, haircuts, health") and it sells the *ritual*, not the *astronomy* [VERIFIED https://www.bustle.com/life/moonly-astrology-app-review]. Co-Star won on tone (terse, personal push copy; "known for daily push notifications … unconventional tone") and on social graph, then lost goodwill when it gated friends' compatibility and pushed 2–3x/day [SECONDARY https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co%E2%80%93Star ; https://unstar.app/blog/co-star-sanctuary-pattern-nebula-stellium-astrology-apps-ranked-2026]. Nebula won on compatibility quizzes and human chat, ~98% relationship queries [VERIFIED TNW].
- **Native Western hooks that map onto our engine:** "lunar day" exists in Western/Russian lunar-day traditions (Moonly's 1–30 lunar days are literally tithis); the **28 Arabic/medieval lunar mansions** (manāzil, used by Agrippa/Picatrix for electional magic) are nakshatra-equivalents; **void-of-course Moon** is the mainstream Western "don't start anything now" rule (iLuna, Lunation); **Moon-sign gardening / biodynamic (Maria Thun) and Farmers' Almanac "Best Days"** are agrarian electional practice with their own buyers [SECONDARY https://www.lunarium.co.uk/apps/iluna/]. Framing for a global mode should therefore be "Moon mansion / lunar day / void Moon", not "nakshatra / tithi / Rahu kaal".
- **Appropriation / dilution risk:** there is a documented precedent in the Hindu American Foundation's "Take Back Yoga" ("yoga has thrived, but Hinduism has lost control of the brand") and the scholarly pushback against it [VERIFIED https://www.npr.org/transcripts/134822766 ; https://openthemagazine.com/features/living/not-as-old-as-you-think]. Inside Jyotish, the purist complaint is the opposite direction — Western astrologers "dilute" Vedic practice [SECONDARY https://www.navamsa.com/?page_id=154]. No HAF/Hinduism Today statement specifically on Vedic astrology apps was found [GAP]. The practical risk for a Toronto Indian-founded company is low on appropriation and higher on *dilution of the Hindu product's credibility* if a "Western-friendly" layer overwrites tradition-specific rules — keep the two modes separate in UI and copy.
- **Store-review themes:** Moonly: love the lunar calendar/dream analysis/interface, hate the paywall and iOS-only features (iOS 4.6★ vs Play 4.0★). Co-Star: love the voice and friends' charts, hate paywall creep, push spam, vagueness, and the recent AI shift (Play ~3.9★). Nebula: 96% one-star on Trustpilot, all billing. Numia: trial with no opt-out. Pattern of the category: **the single biggest Western complaint about astrology apps is billing, not astrology.** A no-dark-pattern, no-ads product is itself a differentiator (same as Research 01 found for India).

## 7. Risks

1. **Apple Guideline 4.3 (Spam).** Current text: "the App Store has enough fart, burp, flashlight, **fortune telling**, dating, drinking games, and Kama Sutra apps" [VERIFIED https://daringfireball.net/linked/2025/11/26/november-update-to-the-app-store-review-guidelines]. Rejection notices quote "primarily features astrology, horoscopes, palm reading, fortune telling or zodiac reports … duplicates the content and functionality of many other similar apps"; Struck (astrology dating) needed ~10 submissions [VERIFIED https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/20/after-numerous-rejections-strucks-dating-app-for-the-co-star-crowd-hits-the-app-store/amp]. A 2026 third-party guide says repeated low-effort submissions in saturated categories can now cost Developer Program membership [UNVERIFIED https://appstorereview.app/guides/apple-app-review-low-value-apps-2026]. Mitigation: a *calendar/panchang utility* with computation shown (tithi/nakshatra tables, timezone-correct festivals) reads as "Reference/Utilities", not "fortune telling"; a generic English horoscope skin does not. This is the strongest technical argument against scenario (c).
2. **Google Play.** No astrology-specific ban; subscription policy forbids misleading offers and "deceptive or manipulative purchase experiences" [VERIFIED https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533?hl=en]. Low risk for a transparent product.
3. **Consumer protection.** FTC v. Genesis Tech (Nebula named; TRO; ROSCA) Jul 2026 [VERIFIED]; ASA guidance for psychic/astrology ads: no promises of health/wealth outcomes, no "accurate/truthful readings" claims, testimonial rules [VERIFIED https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/psychics-spiritualists-fortune-tellers-astrologers-and-clairvoyants.html]; no ASA ruling against an astrology *app* found [GAP]. India CCPA can fine ₹10 lakh for definitive promises [SECONDARY https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/biz-of-belief-how-indias-astrology-apps-are-turning-faith-into-fortune-amid-rising-regulatory-concerns-83027.htm]. Turkey: Law 677 arrest of Faladdin's founder [VERIFIED]. Canada: Competition Act drip-pricing and Quebec's auto-renewal rules apply to any subscription [not researched here — flag for counsel if subscriptions launch].
4. **China.** Android stores require a Chinese entity (WFOE/JV) for ICP + app filing (mandatory since 2023); Apple China ambiguous but trending toward requiring an ICP number [SECONDARY https://www.hicom-asia.com/launch-app-china-app-stores-icp-filing/ ; https://appinchina.co/blog/how-can-i-publish-on-the-apple-app-store-china/]. Not feasible for a solo Canadian corp; overseas-Chinese (SG/MY/HK/TW/CA) is the only reachable huangli audience.
5. **Trademark.** No "ShubhDate"/"Shubh Date" mark or app surfaced; nearby: "Shubh" (muhurat app on Play), "Shubh App Dot Com" (matrimony), SHUBHKART (US class 35) [VERIFIED search https://trademark.justia.com/904/30/shubhkart-90430708.html]. **[GAP: run USPTO TESS, CIPO, IP India and EUIPO for SHUBHDATE and the Devanagari form before spending on a global name.]** "Shubh" is descriptive in Hindi; expect weak protection in India, fine in Canada/US. A global brand would need a second name anyway (nobody in Ohio can say or search "Shubh").
6. **Platform concentration.** Co-Star just sold to Midjourney (Jul 2026) [VERIFIED Bloomberg]; expect AI-native astrology features from a well-funded owner, and Nebula/Hint already run AI chat. A content-horoscope play against them is a losing fight for a solo founder; a correct, transparent *computation* play is not what they do.

## 8. Verdict inputs: three scenarios

All 12-month ranges below are **my estimates [UNVERIFIED]**, derived from the comparables cited; they assume a solo founder, no paid UA, PWA + possible TWA, launch by early 2027 ahead of the Nov 2026–Mar 2027 Indian wedding season (Research 01 §3).

### (a) India + diaspora only (current plan)
- **Evidence for:** the need is culturally owned and seasonal; incumbent (Drik) is ad-heavy and date-shifts in US time zones; diaspora pays NA prices (RevenueCat NA medians) for an Indian need with zero translation; Astrotalk international 20%→37% of revenue proves diaspora willingness to pay; 9 Indian languages already shipped; Apple 4.3 risk is lowest for a "Hindu calendar" utility.
- **Evidence against:** India converts at 0.7% D35 and $18/yr median; ad-free + free means revenue depends on one-off reports/premium only; Drik's 11.7M visits/mo is a big incumbent.
- **12-month range:** 15K–120K installs/PWA adds (diaspora-heavy if SEO targets US/CA/UK date-shift queries); revenue **$0–$8K** without a paid SKU, **$5K–$40K** with a ₹299/$9.99 personal-muhurta report + a $9.99–$19.99/yr diaspora ad-free/premium tier (assumes 1–3% of diaspora users buy once; cf. SmartPuja ₹299, Moonly $14.99 one-time).
- **Product cost:** ~0 incremental; the work is SEO pages per city/time zone and the paid report.

### (b) India core + an English "Moon & Good-Day" mode on the same engine (recommended as phase 2, gated)
- **What it is:** a toggle that hides Sanskrit terms and sidereal signs; shows Moon phase, lunar day 1–30 (tithi renamed), "Moon mansion" (nakshatra renamed, with the 28-mansion Western names optional), void-of-course Moon, Mercury retrograde, and a traffic-light "good day for: signing / moving / launching / travel / haircut" computed from the same rules; optional culture packs (Chinese 宜忌 for overseas-Chinese, rokuyō, Hebrew Omer) as pure calendar arithmetic.
- **Evidence for:** the Western demand that matches our math is the "moon phase today" cluster (242K/mo US head term) and Moonly's lunar-day format (6M downloads, ~$200K/mo); Moonly's own copy proves tithi-like "lunar day energy" sells in plain English; NA conversion is 4x India; the engine already computes everything except VoC aspects; Etsy/Knot show a small but real "good day" curiosity market; no incumbent does *multi-culture* good-day computation.
- **Evidence against:** head-term SEO is owned by moongiant/timeanddate/Yahoo; Moonly, Lunation, iLuna already own the niche inside the App Store; Western users do not search "auspicious date" (gap — verify with Ahrefs); every dollar of Western spend in this category goes to relationship content and human chat, not to calendars; mixing modes risks diluting the Hindu product's credibility and inviting 4.3 scrutiny if wrapped natively.
- **12-month range (incremental):** 5K–40K additional English-mode users, mostly web/SEO; revenue **$1K–$15K** (a $14.99 one-time "your lunar year" report or $19.99/yr premium at 1–2% of English users). Upside only if a creator or a Pinterest/Etsy printable loop catches.
- **Product cost:** 3–6 evenings-weeks: terminology layer (i18n key set "en-global"), VoC aspect calc, 2–3 culture rule tables, a separate landing page + sitemap, App Store/Play listing copy that leads with "Moon calendar" not "astrology". No new languages.
- **Gate:** ship only after (a) shows ≥30% week-4 retention among diaspora users and the paid report converts; otherwise it is a distraction from the wedding-season window.

### (c) Separately branded global app sharing the engine
- **Evidence for:** cleanest brand story ("Shubh" is unpronounceable abroad); NA economics; Midjourney/Co-Star shows strategic buyers exist for astrology UX.
- **Evidence against:** every comparable that reached revenue had capital (Co-Star $20.9M, Sanctuary $6.5M, Nebula 120+ staff and a quiz-funnel machine) and sold *relationship content or humans*; the category's moat is paid UA + creators, not computation; FTC action and Trustpilot 1.3 show where the money came from; Apple 4.3 treats a new generic astrology app as spam by default; two store listings, two support queues, two brands for one person; China unreachable; trademark search still pending.
- **12-month range:** 10K–100K installs only with $10–30K paid UA and TikTok creator seeding; organic alone 3K–20K; revenue **$2K–$40K**, negative after UA. Comparable floor: moon-phase utilities at 1M+ installs over years earn ~$10K/mo at best.
- **Product cost:** 8–16 weeks: brand, domain, native wrapper to be chart-eligible, separate ASO, Western content calendar, legal review of subscription flows in US/CA/UK; ongoing content.

### Decision rule
Do (a) now. Prepare (b) as an i18n/terminology layer so it costs days, not months, and ship it only when (a)'s retention and paid-report data are in. Do not do (c) as a solo founder on Sonnet/Haiku budgets; if the engine proves accurate and a global partner (a Moonly-type app, a wedding platform, or a calendar app wanting a "good days" feature) appears, license the engine instead of building the brand.
